03 · HOW MOMENTUM WORKS

One physical event becomes an enterprise service.

01Connected sourceV16, worker, cone, rescue and work-vehicle nodes connect through source adapters.
02Authenticated ingestionSource identity, schema validation and traceable receipt before the event enters the platform.
03Canonical eventRaw payload is preserved and normalized into one reusable Momentum event model.
04DistributionRead-only API, signed webhooks and adapters deliver the same event to enterprise systems.
05Operational layerRoad Command, My Cluster and customer FMS turn the event into action and visibility.
