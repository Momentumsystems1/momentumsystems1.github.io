# Momentum Systems — Corporate Web Design Contract

Date: 2026-11-14
Owner: Momentum Systems S.L.
Scope: public-facing corporate website and preview.

## Non-negotiable delivery standard
- Never present a visually polished concept as though it were already implemented in the published website.
- Visual design and coded preview must match in layout, image treatment, typographic hierarchy and interaction intent.
- Do not approve a delivery based only on source code or screenshots of static mockups. Check rendered desktop and mobile outputs.
- Clearly differentiate implemented, visually checked, and still pending.
- No fake metrics, partners, warehouse claims, certifications, project maturity or technology performance.
- Respect ZKOSEMI as an external specialist manufacturer, with Momentum Systems presented as exclusive distributor in Spain and distributor in European markets, not as ZKOSEMI's parent company.
- MCLUSTER, PC² and Worker Safety must be labelled with truthful stages.
- Existing specialist product sites remain separate but discoverable through Momentum.

## Visitor experience
- Image-led, compact copy, no walls of text.
- Clear hierarchy: Enterprise Supply / Technology & Mobility / Research & Innovation.
- Meaningful micro-interactions, accessible sliders and unobtrusive scroll effects.
- Large imagery and generous spacing; use deliberate art direction and consistent photography.
- Every card must have a meaningful destination, technical details or request-for-quote path.
- Motion must respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Navigation and forms must work on desktop, tablet and mobile.
- No broken images or dead-end links; honest fallbacks.
- Fast loading and no dependency on non-essential animations.

## Release gate
1. Source-level audit: navigation targets, IDs, responsive styles and accessibility semantics.
2. Rendered 1440px desktop screenshot compared against art direction.
3. Rendered 390px mobile screenshot, menu and carousel checks.
4. Real browser interactions: sliders, modal, links, contact flow and keyboard behavior.
5. Verify externally hosted images and target links.
6. Clearly state what remains illustrative, draft or not deployed.

## AI / tools policy
- Use AI design assistance for visual exploration, not as an excuse to substitute a render for implemented UI.
- Use real engineering/code implementation in GitHub.
- Use image generation or licensed media with assets integrated into the delivered website.
- Test with browser automation and manual visual review where available.
- Specialist AI tools (e.g. visual generators) are optional; choose them only for demonstrably better quality.
